In "Composing Parameter-Efficient Modules with Arithmetic Operations," we demonstrate that parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) modules can be composed via arithmetic operations to transfer learned knowledge across tasks. Our approach opens up new ways to build compositional model adaptations and shows that linearly combining task modules can yield competitive performance on unseen task combinations.
