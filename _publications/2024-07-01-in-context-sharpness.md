In "In-Context Sharpness as Alerts: An Inner Representation Perspective for Hallucination Mitigation," we analyze the internal representations of large language models when generating hallucinated content. We find that hallucinated responses produce unusually sharp token representations, and use this observation to build an early warning system. We then design targeted interventions at inference time to significantly reduce hallucination rates without requiring model retraining.
