In "On the Universal Truthfulness Hyperplane Inside LLMs," we identify a universal linear subspace within the residual stream of large language models that linearly separates true and false statements across model layers. We demonstrate that this truthfulness hyperplane emerges spontaneously during pre-training and can be effectively used to steer model outputs toward truthfulness through controlled vector arithmetic.
