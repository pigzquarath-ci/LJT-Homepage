SynLogic is a large-scale data synthesis framework designed to create verifiable reasoning datasets for training large language model reasoners. We generate diverse logical reasoning problems across multiple domains and use them to train LLMs via reinforcement learning, demonstrating significant improvements in logical reasoning capabilities.
