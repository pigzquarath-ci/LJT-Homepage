C-Eval is a comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models, containing 52 multiple-choice subjects spanning a wide range of disciplines and difficulty levels. As one of the most widely used benchmarks for Chinese language models, C-Eval has enabled systematic evaluation of model capabilities across knowledge domains and has supported the development of improved Chinese foundation models.
