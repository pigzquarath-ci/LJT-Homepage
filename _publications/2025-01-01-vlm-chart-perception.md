On the Perception Bottleneck of VLMs for Chart Understanding investigates how vision-language models perceive and understand visual information from charts. We identify a significant perception bottleneck in current VLMs where they fail to accurately parse chart elements, and propose targeted approaches to enhance chart understanding.
