## About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am advised by [Professor Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/) in the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/). I previously received my Bachelor of Engineering degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in 2024, also advised by Professor Junxian He.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on **natural language processing (NLP)** and **machine learning**, with a particular emphasis on:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**: Exploring how large language models acquire and utilize reasoning capabilities, and applying reinforcement learning techniques to improve their logical and mathematical reasoning
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**: Investigating the perception bottleneck in VLMs and developing methods to reduce hallucinations, particularly in multi-modal reasoning tasks
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**: Studying the internal representation structures that encode truthfulness in LLMs, and developing approaches to make model behaviors more interpretable and reliable
